## What's on our Discord

Discord is the fastest way to take part. It's where we think out loud, ask and answer questions, run our weekly community call, and post **what we need help with**. You don't have to contribute code or content to belong here — come and talk.

A few of the channels you'll find:

- **General** — introductions and open conversation.
- **Thinking · Resources · Research** — discussion around each area of the work.
- **Events** — the weekly call and other sessions.
- **Help wanted** — concrete things we'd love a hand with.
