## What's on our Discord?

**Welcome**

- **⚖️ | rules** — how we keep things friendly.
- **👋 | introductions** — say hello and tell us what brought you here.

**General**

- **📜 | official** — official info and verified core members' Discord IDs.
- **📣 | announcements** — the important news.
- **💬 | general** — open conversation.
- **🤙 | calls** — the voice channel where our weekly call happens.

**Conversations** — forum channels, where discussion lives as threads:

- **🚨 | help-wanted** — concrete things we'd love a hand with, each a post you can pick up.
- **🧠 | thinking** — our point of view: essays and the manifesto.
- **📚 | resources** — the wiki, glossary, and library.
- **🔬 | research** — observations, insights, hypotheses, and reports.
- **🖥️ | tech** — the website, apps, and tools we build, plus tech in general — what's happening in the world of tech and AI.
